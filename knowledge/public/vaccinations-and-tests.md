# Vaccinations and tests

## Adult vaccinations

Routine adult vaccinations are given on Tuesdays and Fridays between 17:00 and 19:00.
Bring your vaccination record. No appointment is needed for a vaccination; check in at
reception and you will be called in turn.

## Blood tests

Blood samples are taken Monday to Saturday from 9:00 to 11:00. Fasting tests need 10 to 12
hours without food; water is fine. Reports are ready by 18:00 the same day.

## What we do not do here

The clinic does not perform X-rays, ultrasound or ECG on site. The doctor will refer you to
a partner centre and reception will help you book there.
