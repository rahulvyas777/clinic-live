# Fees and payment

## Consultation fees

| Visit | Fee |
|---|---|
| General consultation | 600 |
| Follow-up within 7 days of a consultation | 300 |
| Senior citizens (60 and above), general consultation | 450 |

When two fees could apply, the lower one applies. A senior citizen on a follow-up within
7 days pays the follow-up fee, 300.

## Payment

Cash, UPI and cards are accepted at reception. A receipt is printed for every payment and
can also be sent through the app.

## Insurance

The clinic does not bill insurance directly. Reception can stamp and sign a receipt for you
to claim from your insurer.

## Cancellations

Cancelling is free up to 2 hours before the slot. Cancel from the app or by calling
reception. After three missed appointments without cancelling, future bookings need a phone
confirmation on the day.
