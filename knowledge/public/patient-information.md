# Sunrise Family Clinic — patient information

## Opening hours

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 13:00 and 17:00 to 20:30. It is closed on
Sunday and on public holidays. The reception phone is answered during opening hours only.

## Before your appointment

Please arrive 10 minutes before your slot. Bring your appointment code and a photo ID.
First-time patients should also bring any previous prescriptions and reports so the doctor
can see your history.

## Checking in

Check in at the kiosk by the entrance with your appointment code, or at reception if you
prefer. The waiting-room board shows the queue; you will be called by name.

## Reports

Blood test reports are ready by 18:00 on the same day. Collect them at reception or receive
them in the app. Other reports take one to three working days; reception will tell you when.

## Parking

Two-wheelers park in the basement. Cars use the municipal lot opposite the clinic, which is
paid. There is no car parking on the clinic premises.

## Emergencies

The clinic does not provide emergency services. In an emergency call 108.
