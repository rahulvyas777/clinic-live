# Suppliers and internal rates (staff only)

## Partner centres for referrals

| Service | Partner | Phone | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-ray and ultrasound | Northgate Imaging | +00-555-0142 | Same-day slots before 12:00 |
| ECG | Riverside Diagnostics | +00-555-0177 | Report in 24 hours |
| Physiotherapy | Meadow Physio | +00-555-0190 | Tuesdays and Thursdays |

## Internal rates (not for patients)

- Partner imaging is billed to the patient by the partner; the clinic charges nothing.
- Home sample collection through Riverside costs the clinic 150 per visit; charge the patient
  200 and record the difference as collection income.
- Staff and their immediate family pay the follow-up rate for any consultation.

## Consumables

Reorder gloves, swabs and sample tubes when the cupboard reaches one box. The supplier is
Meridian Medical Supplies, order by email through the practitioner; delivery takes two days.

## Who to call

- Server or kiosk not working: the IT contact in the day book, not the supplier.
- Card terminal not working: the bank's merchant line printed on the terminal.
