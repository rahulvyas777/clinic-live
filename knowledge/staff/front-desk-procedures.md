# Front-desk procedures (staff only)

## Opening the desk

1. Switch on the kiosk and the waiting-room board; both should show today's date.
2. Open the queue page and confirm the first three appointments are present.
3. Count the cash float; it should be 2,000. Note any difference in the day book.

## Check-in problems

- A patient without a code: search by phone number on the appointments page, then check
  them in from there. Do not create a second booking.
- A code that is not accepted: the appointment is either for another day or was cancelled.
  Read the appointment page before telling the patient.
- Kiosk frozen: press and hold the reset button for five seconds; the page reloads in about
  ten seconds. Check the patient in at reception meanwhile.

## Calling patients

The practitioner calls the next patient from the queue page. Reception does not call patients
except when the practitioner asks. If a called patient does not appear within two minutes,
mark them as skipped; they return to the top of the queue when they check in again.

## No-shows

Mark a no-show fifteen minutes after the slot. After the third no-show, set the phone
confirmation flag on the patient's record; future bookings then need a call on the day.

## Cash and receipts

Every payment gets a printed receipt. UPI payments are confirmed on the terminal before the
receipt is printed. Refunds need the practitioner's initials in the day book.

## Closing the desk

1. Reconcile cash against receipts; note the difference, if any, in the day book.
2. Put the float in the safe. Deposit anything above the float.
3. Switch off the kiosk and the board. Leave the server on.
